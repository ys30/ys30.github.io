A 1-km spatial decision framework showing where biomass supply, infrastructure and CO₂-storage access align—and where they do not.

01 / 03 · Spatial recommendation

Recommendation tiers

The spatial ranking concentrates the strongest deployment recommendations toward eastern Scotland, where CO₂-storage access and infrastructure connectivity align more favorably.

02 / 03 · System design

Greedy three-hub selection

The multi-facility solution distributes three hubs across eastern, central and southern Scotland rather than simply choosing the three highest independent cells, reducing overlap in biomass catchments.

03 / 03 · Feasibility

Combined suitability and constraints

The combined spatial mask shows how candidate locations emerge only after resource, infrastructure, transport and environmental constraints are evaluated together at 1-km resolution.

What drives the map

CO₂-storage accessibility is the strongest spatial constraint. Eastern and coastal locations benefit from shorter access to North Sea storage, while western locations can face several hundred kilometres of additional transport distance. Infrastructure connectivity can reinforce or weaken that pattern.

Robustness

Increasing the candidate set from 800 to 30,000 improves spatial detail without overturning the core hotspot pattern. By contrast, removing St Fergus, gas-pipeline access or licensed storage fragments or relocates high-suitability areas, showing that infrastructure assumptions materially affect siting.

System-level result

A greedy multi-facility optimization selects three spatially distributed hubs across eastern, central and southern Scotland, reducing biomass-catchment overlap relative to choosing the top three sites independently.

Trade-offs

There is no single universally optimal map. Carbon-dominant weights pull facilities toward the east coast, balanced weights expand central options, and biomass-dominant weights move suitability inland and west. The useful output is therefore a transparent decision surface, not a single black-box score.
