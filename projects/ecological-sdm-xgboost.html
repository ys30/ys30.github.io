Technical details

Expanded statistical comparisons, tuning caveats, and reproduction notes are available below and in the repository.

Historical matched model comparison

The original matched three-pipeline analysis evaluated 23 eligible taxon–fold cells across five taxa: pooled XGBoost AUC 0.8016, MaxEnt 0.7865, hierarchical Bayesian 0.7610. Different feature sets mean this is a pipeline comparison, not an isolated algorithm effect.

Rare-taxon decision support

At 25 plots per fold, 125/1,167 plots (10.7%) were selected in total. Bayesian retrospective detection recovery was 3/8 (nsw19), 17/18 (nsw20), and 4/6 (nsw22). Observed non-detections are not detection-corrected absences.

Spatial uncertainty, calibration and limitations

Second-partition and buffered sensitivity analyses compared model discrimination, proper probability scores and residual spatial autocorrelation. Eight-taxon bootstrap intervals are exploratory, and some rare taxon–fold cells do not support defined AUC. Independent-region validation, prospective field outcomes, and full v6.25b CI reproduction remain open.
