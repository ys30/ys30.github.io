From model output to a usable product

I designed and deployed an interactive R Shiny GUI that joins CMIP6 climate projections, socioeconomic data and RCCDAS integrated-assessment outputs. Users can move across regions, sectors and development-climate pathways without manually working through the underlying model output.

Applied outcome

The platform supported RCCDAS software adoption for government use, translating integrated-assessment results into scenario-based visualizations for climate-risk and adaptation planning.

Analytical backbone

The broader RCCDAS workflow links CGE, GIS and CMIP6 projections and enables systematic cross-sector and cross-scenario comparison. Reproducible pipelines organize climate, socioeconomic and sector data before they reach the visualization layer.
