Case study

The question

Interactive climate-risk decision support connecting climate projections, socioeconomic pathways, sector impacts and adaptation-oriented outputs.

Data

CMIP6 climate projections, socioeconomic scenario data, and modeled sector outputs across 3 provinces, 14 sectors and 4 SSP-RCP scenarios.

Approach

I organized heterogeneous climate and socioeconomic outputs into a consistent analytical structure and built an interactive R Shiny layer for exploring scenarios, sectors and regional differences. The emphasis was not only computation, but making complex model output inspectable and useful for decision support.

What this demonstrates

The resulting workflow supports comparison across provinces, sectors and development-climate pathways, turning a large model-output space into a navigable evidence product.

Why it matters

This work reflects my broader approach to environmental data science: connect domain knowledge with careful data engineering, choose methods that fit the scientific question, make assumptions visible, and translate analytical output into evidence that can support decisions.
