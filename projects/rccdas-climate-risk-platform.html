From climate signals to decision support

I designed and deployed an interactive R Shiny platform that organizes refreshed climate and economic model outputs into a single analytical chain. Users can compare climate trajectories, climate-damage channels, macroeconomic consequences, model uncertainty and adaptation experiments without manually working through high-dimensional model outputs.

Current product

The deployed dashboard includes Overview, Climate Outlook, Climate Damages, Economic Impacts, Uncertainty & Robustness, Executive Insights, Adaptation Explorer, and Methods & Data sections.

Analytical backbone

The current platform connects six climate models and ensemble summaries with region-specific economic results for Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei. Climate-model spread is separated from economic-model robustness so users can see where uncertainty enters the decision process rather than treating the analysis as a single deterministic projection.

Research data note

The associated manuscript is not yet published, so the underlying research datasets and full model outputs are not distributed through the public GitHub repository at this stage. The live dashboard provides the current research-facing visualization and decision-support interface.
