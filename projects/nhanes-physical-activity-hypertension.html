Physical Activity, Adiposity & Hypertension

A reproducible NHANES workflow examining whether waist circumference helps explain the relationship between moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and hypertension, while accounting for demographic and lifestyle covariates.

The question

How is moderate-to-vigorous physical activity associated with hypertension, and does central adiposity—as measured by waist circumference—help explain that relationship?

Data engineering

The workflow downloads and joins NHANES demographic, body-measure, blood-pressure, physical-activity, smoking, alcohol and blood-pressure questionnaire files. Physical activity is constructed from work, transport and recreational activity; hypertension is derived from repeated systolic and diastolic measurements. The analysis focuses on adults ages 18–62 with complete covariate information and excludes participants reporting blood-pressure medication use.

Modeling strategy

The analysis uses a three-model pathway: a linear model for physical activity → waist circumference, a logistic model for physical activity → hypertension, and a second logistic model adding waist circumference. Models adjust for age, sex, race/ethnicity, income-to-poverty ratio, sedentary time, smoking and alcohol. Model comparison, AIC, VIF, ROC/AUC, Hosmer–Lemeshow and influence diagnostics are included in the workflow.

Portfolio scope
