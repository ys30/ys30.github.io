Ecological Modeling with XGBoost

A machine-learning workflow for translating environmental predictors into spatially interpretable species-occurrence models.

RoleModeling & spatial analysis
ScopeSpecies distribution
MethodsPython · XGBoost · GIS
FocusDecision-relevant evidence
Case study

The question

A machine-learning workflow for translating environmental predictors into spatially interpretable species-occurrence models.

Data

Species-occurrence information paired with environmental and spatial predictor layers.

Approach

The workflow combines predictor preprocessing, XGBoost modeling, performance evaluation and spatial interpretation. The goal is to use flexible nonlinear prediction while keeping the ecological meaning of predictors and mapped outputs visible.

What this demonstrates

The project demonstrates an applied bridge between ecological questions, machine learning and GIS—using predictive modeling as part of a spatial scientific workflow rather than as an isolated benchmark.

Why it matters

This work reflects my broader approach to environmental data science: connect domain knowledge with careful data engineering, choose methods that fit the scientific question, make assumptions visible, and translate analytical output into evidence that can support decisions.
