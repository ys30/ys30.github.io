Green Space, PM2.5 & Mental Health

Testing whether greener environments are associated with lower depressive-symptom scores across five waves of CHARLS, while separating spatial, temporal and air-pollution effects.

NDVI QA design

Pixel Reliability is retained as an explicit sensitivity dimension. Good + Marginal observations provide a less destructive QA screen, while Good-only filtering is treated as a strict sensitivity check because it creates large spatial gaps. The exposure pipeline also records valid-pixel counts so coverage can be audited before model fitting.

Results at a glance

What the results say

The primary spatial-and-wave adjusted model shows an inverse NDVI association. That pattern also persists when city-specific trends are added, but it attenuates in the smaller complete-case sample after adding household consumption. I therefore present the result as specification-sensitive evidence rather than a single causal estimate.

PM2.5 diagnostic

The initially counterintuitive inverse PM2.5 association largely disappears after stronger temporal controls: the within-city PM2.5 coefficient is approximately null once both spatial and time fixed effects are included. This is an important indication of temporal confounding rather than a protective pollution effect.

Data & design

Harmonized CHARLS person-wave records are linked to monthly 1-km MODIS MOD13A3 NDVI, annual MCD12Q1 land-cover masks, city-level PM2.5, geographic identifiers, and community/household covariates. The exposure workflow spans 2005–2020, creating city × urban/non-urban × month NDVI records before deriving lagged and longer-term exposure windows. Models then add spatial and survey-wave controls, participant/community random intercepts, and progressively stronger temporal specifications.

Current interpretation

Greenness appears associated with fewer depressive symptoms in the primary specification, but effect size and significance depend on socioeconomic completeness and time-control choices. Earlier lag analyses show the strongest inverse estimate at lag 2; the full lag set is still being re-run and is not treated as a final headline result here.
