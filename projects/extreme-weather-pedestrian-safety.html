Extreme Weather & Pedestrian Safety

Spatiotemporal data integration for examining how flooding, snow and other weather conditions intersect with pedestrian safety.

RoleData science & verification
ScopeSpatiotemporal analysis
MethodsPython/R · weather data · QA/QC
FocusDecision-relevant evidence
Case study

The question

Spatiotemporal data integration for examining how flooding, snow and other weather conditions intersect with pedestrian safety.

Data

Weather observations and pedestrian-accident records assembled for aligned temporal and spatial analysis.

Approach

The workflow focuses on data verification, event alignment and quality control before modeling. Weather and accident records are harmonized so exposure conditions can be compared consistently across events and locations.

What this demonstrates

The project creates an analysis-ready foundation for evaluating weather-related safety patterns and illustrates the importance of verification and QA/QC in applied climate-risk analytics.

Why it matters

This work reflects my broader approach to environmental data science: connect domain knowledge with careful data engineering, choose methods that fit the scientific question, make assumptions visible, and translate analytical output into evidence that can support decisions.
