Extreme Weather & Pedestrian Safety

Building an analysis-ready link between weather conditions and pedestrian-accident records to reveal interpretable risk patterns around flooding, snow and other extremes.

What the project produced

The project created a reproducible, analysis-ready linkage between weather observations and pedestrian-accident records. The main analytical value is the ability to compare accident patterns under flooding, snow and other extreme-weather conditions with consistent temporal and spatial alignment.

Why verification matters

Weather-risk analyses are highly sensitive to event timing, location matching and missing or inconsistent records. I treated data verification and QA/QC as part of the scientific result rather than a preprocessing footnote, so downstream risk patterns remain interpretable.

Current evidence boundary

The archived portfolio and resume materials verify the data-integration workflow and its extreme-weather focus, but the original paper/result figure has not yet been recovered from the Library. I therefore do not show a quantitative effect size or substitute a synthetic map on this page.
