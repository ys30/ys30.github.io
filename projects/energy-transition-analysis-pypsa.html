Wind accounting: a useful research finding

The 2024 EIA-923 generation of 28 wind plants mapped to BPAT by EIA-860 totals 8,820,435.41 MWh; EIA-930 hourly BPAT wind totals 6,839,734 MWh. The descriptive difference is 1,980,701.41 MWh. The EIA-930A reporting-population audit found 20 of the 28 plant IDs represented in BPAT's Schedule 2 inventory and 8 absent, with the latter corresponding to 1,802,779 MWh of EIA-923 generation.

This provides evidence that plant/BA reporting populations may differ. It does not prove that those 1,802,779 MWh explain an equivalent portion of the gap: EIA-930A records generators, while this comparison is plant-level, and operational reporting boundaries may differ. See the official population audit and cross-source reconciliation.

What the battery scenario actually shows

A separate 8,784-hour PyPSA study compares a hypothetical 1-GW/4-GWh battery against matched no-battery scenarios. Modeled unmet demand falls from 73,373 to 51,021 MWh, a reduction of 22,352 MWh (30.5%). Across three treatments for 55 imputed renewable observations, these modeled dispatch results were unchanged under the study assumptions. The reported $315.99 million operating-objective difference contains $223.52 million in assumed shortage penalties, depends on a $10,000/MWh shortage penalty, and excludes battery capital expenditure and degradation. It is not verified project profitability or a real WECC reliability gain. Full scenario assumptions and numerical evidence.

PyPSA network-optimization capability

A separate synthetic two-bus PyPSA/HiGHS linear optimization tests congestion and dispatch: 25, 50 and 125 MW line ratings yield 25, 50 and 100 MW solved flows and 75, 50 and 0 MW of backup generation. This demonstrates solver behavior, not verified historical WECC branch flows or actual system savings. Review the solver evidence.

Methods and technical skills

Limitations and next phase

These delivered components support a research-engineering portfolio, not a calibrated interconnected western U.S. power-system model. Verified 2024 WECC line topology, electrical impedance and thermal ratings, accurate BA operational accounting and physical historical validation remain outstanding. Future work will construct a source-authenticated network and rerun renewable/storage scenarios only after those evidence gates are met.
