Sector-Specific Climate Damage Functions

Multi-model climate-economy analysis designed to retain differences in climate damages across sectors and development pathways.

RoleLead analysis & manuscript
ScopeSector-specific · multi-model

FocusDecision-relevant evidence
Case study

The question

Multi-model climate-economy analysis designed to retain differences in climate damages across sectors and development pathways.

Data

Climate and socioeconomic scenario inputs combined with sector-level economic modeling for the Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei megaregion.

Approach

Rather than collapsing climate impacts into one aggregate damage relationship, the framework evaluates sector-specific responses across models and scenarios. This preserves heterogeneity that can matter for adaptation planning and interpretation of regional economic risk.

What this demonstrates

The work supports comparison of how climate-related economic impacts vary by sector, model and development pathway. The manuscript is under revision, so this page describes the analytical framework without overstating unpublished results.

Why it matters

This work reflects my broader approach to environmental data science: connect domain knowledge with careful data engineering, choose methods that fit the scientific question, make assumptions visible, and translate analytical output into evidence that can support decisions.
