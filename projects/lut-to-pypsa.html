Research continuation (v2.0)

A separate private research repository, MultiYear-Energy-System-Validation, has been initialized for multi-year observed-data intake, validation contracts, and a manuscript outline. Initial CI tests have passed, but no empirical multi-year results or calibrated grid findings are claimed. This engineering v1.0 repository remains the public, reproducible demonstration.

What the engineering demonstrates

The key deliverable is a reproducible modeling workflow: trace annual input provenance, convert energy to hourly power consistently, build and solve PyPSA dispatch scenarios, hold capacity fixed for controlled comparisons, and test both solver balance and published chart coordinates. Interpretation boundary: synthetic profiles, assumed firm generation, isolated continental buses, and a high-cost shortage-proxy generator; no measured grid outages, transmission, storage, or calibrated policy impact.

Source and geographic validity

An observed-capacity import workflow can construct an unsolved network and audit source year, technology, region mapping and geographic completeness. A partial US inventory cannot be presented as complete capacity for the Americas. No global calibrated optimization or multi-sector LUT equivalence is claimed.
