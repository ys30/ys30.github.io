LUT-to-PyPSA: Energy Demand Scenario Integration

An independent, reproducible workflow for translating source-traced LUT-DEMAND annual electricity service demand into validated PyPSA inputs, with explicit assumptions and model-boundary audits.

Source code & tests ↗CI workflows ↗

Quantitative and technical evidence

Authentic input

2020 · Five continents

Small source-cell-traced LUT-DEMAND v1.1 annual electricity demand fixture. The complete original workbook is not bundled.

Time conversion

8,784 hours

Leap-year 2020 conservation checks for each region; annual-to-hourly load shapes are assumed rather than observed.

Sensitivity

0.90× · 1.00× · 1.10× · 1.25×

Demand multipliers in illustrative fixed-capacity stress tests. The 1.25× case implies 12% unmet demand by construction with 10% baseline reserve margin and flat hourly load.

Model engineering

PyPSA + HiGHS

24-hour illustrative dispatch smoke test, unit conversions, region coverage validation and NetCDF export. Not LUT-ESTM output equivalence.

Source and geographic validity

An observed-capacity import workflow can construct an unsolved network and audit source year, technology, region mapping and geographic completeness. A partial US inventory cannot be presented as complete capacity for the Americas. No global calibrated optimization or multi-sector LUT equivalence is claimed.
