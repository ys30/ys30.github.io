What I deliver

End-to-end environmental analytics, from raw data to usable decisions

I combine domain knowledge, data engineering, spatial analytics and quantitative modeling to build evidence that can be explored, communicated and acted on.

Build reliable data

Integrate environmental, socioeconomic, operational and geospatial sources into analysis-ready pipelines with QA/QC and reproducible workflows.

Measure risk & exposure

Translate climate, remote-sensing and spatial data into interpretable indicators of physical risk, environmental exposure and regional vulnerability.

Model what matters

Use statistical, longitudinal, causal and machine-learning methods to quantify relationships, test robustness and surface decision-relevant signals.

Turn analysis into products

Deliver dashboards, maps, scenario tools and concise technical outputs that make complex results accessible to researchers, operators and decision-makers.

Capabilities

Environmental domain expertise with a data-product mindset.

I work at the intersection of environmental and climate science, data science, and geospatial analytics. I’m most useful when a problem spans multiple data sources and needs both rigorous analysis and a clear, usable output.

My work has covered climate-risk analytics, environmental exposure, remote sensing, regional economic impacts, carbon-removal siting and operational data workflows. Across those domains, I focus on building reproducible pipelines, choosing fit-for-purpose models, validating results and communicating the signal without losing the uncertainty.

My technical toolkit includes Python, R, SQL, GIS and remote sensing, statistical and machine-learning methods, and interactive analytics with R Shiny and BI tools. My background in ecology and environmental policy helps me connect technical outputs to the physical systems and decisions behind them.
