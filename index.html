A little more about me

I like messy environmental questions, clean data, and maps that actually tell you something.

I started in ecology and somehow ended up happily moving between climate models, satellite data, statistics, GIS and interactive dashboards. The common thread is simple: I like figuring out what a complicated dataset is really saying—and then making the answer easier for someone else to use.

My projects have taken me from vegetation and environmental exposure to regional climate economics, pedestrian safety and carbon-removal siting. I’m especially drawn to problems where the data come from five different places, the uncertainty matters, and the final result needs to be more useful than a 40-page methods section.

Most days that means Python, R, SQL and GIS. On a good day, it ends with a map, a model, or a dashboard that makes a difficult question feel surprisingly clear.
