About

Environmental science first. Data as the common language.

I’m an interdisciplinary data and research professional with a PhD in Ecology, an MPA focused on environmental and energy policy, and more than a decade of experience across environmental science, climate economics, geospatial analytics and quantitative decision support.

My path began with field ecology and hydrological variation, then expanded into environmental policy, climate economics, integrated assessment, GIS, remote sensing and applied data science. The consistent thread is converting heterogeneous information into reproducible analysis, validated evidence and clear outputs.

At the University of New Mexico–Los Alamos, I currently work with institutional financial and operational data and build workflow/reporting systems while continuing environmental, climate and health analytics through independent research and collaborations.
